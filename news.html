    Enzara Therapeutics forms to advance Cyst(e)inase for glioblastoma

    Enzara Therapeutics has incorporated as a Delaware corporation to develop Cyst(e)inase, an engineered human enzyme, toward clinical trials in glioblastoma. The company will operate in Texas and Maryland.

  • 2026
    Publication

    iScience publishes Cyst(e)inase study in glioblastoma

    Researchers at UT Austin and the University of Maryland report that systemic Cyst(e)inase induces ferroptosis in glioblastoma models and acts synergistically with temozolomide. Read the paper
